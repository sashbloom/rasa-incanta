# Ideas Treasury — Engagement Ideas to Keep Things Warm & Progress

**Applies across the journey:** Prospect → Pre-Pipeline → Pipeline → Closure.

**Objective:** Stay top-of-mind, deepen trust, and create relevance without forcing a sale.

**How to use this list:** Treat it as a menu, not a script. For each opportunity, pick the 1–2
moves that best fit its stage, industry, owner and history. Earlier-stage opportunities lean on
Thought Leadership and Relationship & Presence; later-stage ones lean on Decision Enablement,
Stakeholder Alignment and Soft Nudges. Add strong new ideas Mahak records over time.

---

## 1. Context-based or Thought Leadership

1. Share short, relevant insights on trends or challenges being discussed by CFOs and CXOs in their industry.
2. Send thoughtful follow-ups after industry events highlighting 2–3 practical takeaways.
3. Offer co-authorship for blogs to be published on LinkedIn or industry publications.
4. Share curated reading material or articles with a short personal context on why it may matter.
5. Share brief video or voice-note insights explaining a common challenge or an industry best practice.
6. Share blogs or opinion pieces that reflect issues the client is likely grappling with.

## 2. Ongoing Relationship & Presence Building

1. Conduct no-agenda check-ins to understand how priorities are evolving.
2. Reconnect with a simple “has anything changed since we last spoke?”
3. Maintain light but consistent engagement during AOP, budgeting, planning, or board cycles.
4. Inform about travel plans (yours or other leaders from Practus) as natural opportunities for informal catch-ups.
5. Acknowledge leadership milestones, role changes, or organisational achievements thoughtfully.
6. Reference something they said earlier to show continuity and listening.
7. Share a “what we are seeing across clients right now” one-pager (monthly–quarterly).
8. Share insights on what boards, investors, or PE funds are currently focusing on.

## 3. Community & Ecosystem Engagement — Build Trust Signals

1. Invite to our events or event-partner events — closed-door CXO roundtables or small, curated leadership discussions.
2. Invite to exclusive learning sessions rather than sales presentations.
3. Invite business leaders to participate in SV's podcasts (CruX).
4. Invite them as a panelist to showcase their leadership voice for relevant events.
5. Connect them to relevant peers, partners, or ecosystem players without expectations.
6. Introduce Venkat or Deepak for a general catch-up to bring in a founder's lens and signal seriousness.
7. Introduce SMEs or advisors to reinforce credibility and presence when relevant (Vamesh, Vivek, Arun, Bimal, or external partners / SMEs).
8. Show up consistently as a calm, reliable thinking partner who adds value even when no immediate work is discussed.

## 4. Decision Enablement & Confidence Building

1. Offer non-billable “dip-sticks” to move things forward and demonstrate intent.
2. Offer limited pre-work (wireframes, dashboards, frameworks) using their data points as samples.
3. Offer to review or sanity-check approaches proposed by other advisors or vendors.
4. Share case studies showing outcomes achieved for similar organisations (without pitching).
5. Share insights on how similar organisations structured the required outputs — specific to their size, sector, or growth stage.
6. Propose a focused working session or problem-definition workshop rather than a proposal discussion.
7. Define success metrics and what “good” looks like before commercial conversations.
8. Help define what not to do in Phase 1 to reduce scope anxiety.
9. Position conversations around outcomes and decision clarity, not tools or deliverables.
10. Offer clarity on risks, trade-offs, and constraints upfront to build credibility.
11. Articulate when Practus may not be the right fit, to reinforce objectivity.
12. Offer short readiness checklists or self-diagnostics to help them assess their current state.

## 5. Stakeholder Alignment

1. Help them think through internal alignment across CFO, CEO, Ops, Tech, or Board expectations.
2. Offer guidance on how to position the initiative internally for approvals or buy-in (what makes each persona look good because of Practus's intervention).
3. Share examples of how peers justified ROI or investment to boards or investors.
4. Demonstrate ROI calculations and parameters to align on potential impact.
5. Bring in the right SME at the right moment to answer specific concerns and navigate the discussions.
6. Share a before-after operating model snapshot — a visual for relatability, possibly with a decision tree outlining implications and pitfalls.

## 6. Soft Nudges — Conversion

1. For penetration — reframe the conversation to testing value in a limited scope (to be discussed internally before proposing).
2. Suggest a phased entry model with a clearly defined exit or scale-up decision point (show flexibility and confidence in delivery and value creation).
3. Anchor the discussion around time-to-value rather than total transformation.
4. Provide references / client testimonials to showcase confidence and ensure credibility.

---

> **Remember:** our objective is not to sell harder, but to help clients think better and decide
> faster. When we do this consistently, conversion becomes a natural outcome.
